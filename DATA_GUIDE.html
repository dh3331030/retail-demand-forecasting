Project guide

What the data represent

The final project carries the 13-file approved baseline from the shared project, unchanged. The manifest records original filenames, byte sizes and SHA-256 fingerprints; the run check verifies the current copies. A matching fingerprint proves local file identity, not external provenance or historical availability.

Retail is the forecasting target

The Eurostat sts_trtu_m custom extract 22913457 contains food, beverage and tobacco retail-volume indices, seasonally and calendar adjusted, with 2021 = 100. This measures sales volume rather than nominal revenue, so a price increase alone is not interpreted as demand growth. The source workbook’s metadata, units and adjustment labels are reproduced in the series dictionary. The forecasting countries are Germany, France, Italy, Poland and Portugal. The extracted retail data retain the index, source flags, calendar month, country and derived growth.

retail_growth_mom = 100 × log(retail_volume[t] / retail_volume[t−1]). No missing target is filled. The script verifies continuous monthly histories before fitting. All available earlier history is retained, while prediction targets stop at December 2025.

Economic context is preserved at its observed frequency

  • GDP: Eurostat namq_10_gdp. The supporting model selects quarter-on-quarter chain-linked volume growth, seasonally and calendar adjusted. At each origin it uses the most recent quarter under an explicit quarter-end-plus-two-month availability assumption.
  • Gini: Eurostat ilc_di12, total age class, equivalised disposable income, scale 0–100. Fifty annual observations cover the five countries over 2016–2025. They are not duplicated into monthly predictors.
  • Inflation: the supplied headline and energy files are byte-identical, and both identify all-items HICP. A separate energy series is therefore unverified. Workbook metadata states percentage change m/m-1; filenames saying annual must not override the actual unit. Available history starts in September 2025.
  • Confidence and unemployment: these extracts start in December and November 2025 respectively, so they do not provide the historical panel needed by the forecast experiment.
  • Household consumption and sector accounts: observations and sheet definitions remain in the long context dataset. They are descriptive source context, not inputs to the selected ARIMA.

Economic observations with series metadata · Coverage by country and series · Annual Gini. Missing values and original flags remain explicit; no API-expanded historical panel was migrated.

Conflict and COVID

UCDP: the approved GED workbook contains 417,968 uniquely identified events. The pipeline groups them by event-start month and sums the recorded best death estimates, also counting the three violence types. Months with no recorded events within the workbook’s covered years are zero; months outside coverage remain unavailable when joined to retail. Missing death values are ignored in the sum, so these totals are recorded estimates rather than complete mortality. A global monthly series is not distance-weighted exposure for each European country. The same global number is shared across countries, so repeated country rows are not independent conflict observations.

The supporting forecast uses log(1 + events) and log(1 + deaths) from two months before the target. The lag is an assumption, not verified historical GED release availability. The comparison measures predictive association and cannot establish that conflict caused retail changes. Global conflict data.

COVID: the national-total rows of OxCGRT_compact_national_v1.csv are extracted from the approved ZIP. Monthly means ignore missing daily values but remain missing where every daily observation is absent. Outside the source’s observed months, values stay missing, not zero. The monthly stringency series is retained for context, not used in the chosen ARIMA or the matched GDP/conflict regression.

Sources and access

The untouched local source register is in data/raw/project_data_sources.xlsx. Eurostat dataset codes and extraction/update information are retained in the series dictionary. Original retrieval dates and source redistribution terms are not fully established by the local record; do not invent them. The approved UCDP workbook and Oxford ZIP are included so this project runs without the old workspace.

Useful fields

  • iso3: DEU, FRA, ITA, POL or PRT.
  • month / target_month: calendar month, YYYY-MM.
  • actual / prediction: observed and predicted monthly retail growth, in log percentage points.
  • training_end: latest month admitted to fitting, always before the target.
  • period in economic data: original month, quarter or year, without artificial frequency conversion.
  • flag: the original workbook observation qualifier; no invented interpretation is substituted.
