EUROPEAN RETAIL DEMAND FORECASTING
PROJECT STRUCTURE AND EXECUTION GUIDE

CURRENT STATUS
Data preparation is implemented in scripts/01_prepare_data.R and saves
the merged dataset as data/processed/prepared_panel.rds.
Model-data construction is implemented through monthly retail growth and
its first two lags in scripts/02_construct_model_data.R. Predictor information
availability, remaining lags, and missing-value rules are still pending.
Training, validation and final evaluation have not been implemented.

HOW TO RESUME
See START_HERE.txt. Extract the whole project before opening the Rproj.
All active paths are relative to the project folder.
Run source("run_development.R") to rebuild preparation, then
source("scripts/02_construct_model_data.R") to reach the last discussed step.
The development runner currently executes preparation only.

CURRENT FILES
Retail_forecasting.Rproj opens the active project in RStudio.
00_setup.R checks the project location and required packages: data.table
and readxl. Modeling packages will be added as models are implemented.
scripts/00_config.R stores shared settings. Sample dates, countries,
prepared-panel path, source sheets and conflict candidates are recorded.
Remaining NULL entries represent modeling settings still to be finalized.
scripts/00_preflight.R checks seven sources, filenames, required Excel
sheets and the national COVID CSV member inside its ZIP.
Inflation uses Sheet 16, GDP Sheet 2, retail and unemployment Sheet 1.
scripts/01_prepare_data.R imports and merges retail, inflation,
unemployment, quarterly GDP converted to monthly equivalents, COVID
stringency and four conflict measures. UCDP columns 48 and 49 are read as
text to preserve multiple codes and avoid numeric-conversion warnings.
The remaining date filters and source paths retain the original explicit
style; further refactoring was not performed.
scripts/02_construct_model_data.R checks monthly continuity and duplicates,
constructs 100 * log(current_index / previous_index), and adds retail growth
lags of one and two months. It retains initial missing values. It does not
save a final modeling dataset or fit models.
run_development.R runs setup, config, preflight and preparation, then reports
successful completion instead of raising the obsolete not-implemented error.
run_final_evaluation.R remains the original guarded placeholder, requiring
a frozen configuration and stopping before unimplemented final evaluation.

CHRONOLOGICAL SAMPLES
Training: January 2015 through December 2022 (480 country-month rows).
Validation: January through December 2023 (60 country-month rows).
Testing: January 2024 through December 2025 (120 country-month rows).
Counts assume five countries and complete monthly coverage. Row counts
do not imply all predictors or the growth target are nonmissing.
Sample membership refers to the month being predicted. Compare model and
conflict definitions in validation; freeze choices before evaluating tests.

COUNTRIES AND CANDIDATES
Germany, France, Italy, Poland and Portugal.
Candidate predictors currently in the prepared panel: inflation_yoy,
unemployment_rate, gdp_monthly_equivalent, covid_stringency, conflict_500,
conflict_1000, conflict_2000 and conflict_deaths_1000.
The four conflict definitions are alternatives to compare during validation,
not a decision to use all four together. Retail growth lags are constructed,
but admissible forecast inputs still depend on publication timing.
Household consumption has not been merged or used.

FOLDERS
scripts/: active numbered R scripts; masters determine execution order.
data/raw/: seven original Excel/ZIP files; six are currently used in the
panel, while household consumption is present but not incorporated.
data/: source registers, which are not counted among the seven raw inputs.
data/processed/: extracted COVID CSV and other archived processed inputs.
prepared_panel_before_split_update.rds is the original archive snapshot,
with old sample labels. Rebuild prepared_panel.rds before using file 02.
results/development/: reserved for validation and frozen specifications.
results/final/: reserved for final testing and reporting.
reference_materials/: tutoring archive and supplied project context PDF.
The uploaded archive did not contain 0_old/ or earlier repository history.

NEXT WORK
Set the forecast origin and information-availability assumptions; construct
remaining predictor lags, resolve post-2022 COVID missingness and select
usable observations. Only then implement training and rolling validation.
Converting GDP to monthly equivalents does not change its quarterly
observation or publication schedule. Historical revised data and conflict
event dates alone do not establish real-time information availability.
