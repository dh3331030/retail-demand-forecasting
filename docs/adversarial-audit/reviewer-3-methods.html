Known design limits: retain the disclosure, do not redesign overnight

  • Historical data are revised: fixed publication lags do not reconstruct the information actually released at each historical date. Annual GED also contains later finalized records.
  • Model selection is retrospective: complete 2023 validation outcomes were unavailable at the first 2024 forecast origins. This is explicitly disclosed in configuration and local report p9. It prevents a strict real-time claim; it does not make a clearly labeled retrospective course exercise invalid.
  • Limited uncertainty evidence: no significance test or forecast-uncertainty analysis establishes the small improvements. Five related countries across 24 months are not 120 independent economic experiments.
  • Selection pressure: 26 specifications were compared on 60 validation observations. The separate holdout is valuable, but new country/model choices made after viewing test performance would require fresh evaluation.

What changes numerically, and what does not

The separate full rerun took 179 seconds and completed. All ten processed RDS comparisons were equivalent at tolerance 10−10. Simple and linear test forecasts agree within about 10−15. All 360 final forest predictions differ from the published run. The maximum individual final prediction difference is 0.123500 log percentage points. The largest aggregate forest RMSE change is 0.005673, for the forest without conflict. The rerun matches the existing unpublished local test predictions; this does not explain the cause of the published-run discrepancy.

The pooled ranking still puts country mean first and macro-only regression second; zero growth remains the validation-selected primary. The matched forest with conflict remains worse in aggregate than the forest without conflict. Every country winner's identity is stable. Therefore the evidence does not overturn the main answer about the limited incremental value of the tested complexity and conflict input.

A year-specific statement does change: published 2025 RMSE is 1.039632 for forest without conflict and 1.041426 for country mean, a forest advantage of only 0.001794. In the local output matching the rerun, forest without conflict is 1.049935 and country mean remains 1.041426, so country mean wins by 0.008509. A slide claiming a 2025 forest winner must be checked against the authoritative final run. Italy's no-GDP forest winner identity is stable but its numerical RMSE also changes.
